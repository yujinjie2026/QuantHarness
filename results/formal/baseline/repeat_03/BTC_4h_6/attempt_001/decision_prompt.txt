### Reasoning
- **Prior trend / context:** Price declines from roughly **27,600** into a low near **26,550–26,700**.
- **Horizontal consolidation:** From about **Oct 11 16:00 to Oct 15 00:00**, price compresses into a relatively flat range around **26,700–27,000**. Candles become smaller and the market moves sideways, forming a base.
- **Sudden upward breakout:** After the flat base, price breaks above the range with strong consecutive bullish candles, accelerating from around **27,000** to a high near **30,000**. This sudden upward resolution after a horizontal base is the defining feature of a **Hidden Base** pattern.
- **Rectangle component:** The base itself is essentially a rectangle: horizontal support near **26,650–26,700** and resistance near **27,000**, followed by an upside breakout.

### Why it is not the other patterns
- **Not a Double Bottom:** There are not two clearly separated significant lows with a meaningful rebound between them.
- **Not an Inverse Head and Shoulders:** There is no clear three-trough structure with a lower middle low.
- **Not a Rounded Bottom:** The base is flat and rectangular, not a gradual U-shaped curve.
- **Not an Ascending Triangle:** Resistance is flat, but support is not clearly rising or converging into an apex.
- **Not a Bullish Flag:** A bullish flag requires a sharp prior rally followed by brief downward consolidation; here the consolidation occurs before the sharp breakout.

**Conclusion:** The chart shows a **Hidden Base / Rectangle consolidation followed by a strong bullish breakout**.
