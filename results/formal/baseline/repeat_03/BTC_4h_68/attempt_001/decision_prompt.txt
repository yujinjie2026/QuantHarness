            **Pattern Report**  
            **Primary match: Hidden Base / base breakout**  
The 4H chart shows a sharp selloff from roughly **70,500** down to the **64,500–65,000** area, followed by a long, relatively horizontal consolidation/base roughly between **65,000 and 66,500**. Price repeatedly holds the lower support zone, then breaks above the base with strong green candles, accelerating toward **68,500**. That horizontal base followed by a sudden upward breakout is the key characteristic of a **Hidden Base** pattern. In this case, it acts as a bullish reversal after the prior decline.

**Secondary resemblance: Bullish Flag / continuation structure**  
After the breakout rally to about **68,500**, price pulls back/consolidates in a choppy range toward **66,500–67,000**, then resumes upward toward **69,500**. This can be viewed as a **bullish flag/continuation**—sharp rally as the flagpole, followed by a pullback consolidation, then continuation higher. The later portion also has some **ascending-triangle-like** behavior, with higher lows pressing into resistance near the high-68k area before breakout.

**Why not the other patterns?**  
It is not a clean **Double Bottom** or **Inverse Head and Shoulders** because the lows are not symmetrical: the early low is much deeper, and the later higher low is well above it. It also is not a textbook **Rounded Bottom** because the left side is a steep decline rather than a gradual curve.

Overall, the chart is best described as a **Hidden Base breakout**, with a later bullish continuation/flag-like move.
